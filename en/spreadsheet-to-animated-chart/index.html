How to turn a spreadsheet into an animated chart

In Datavix you add an .xlsx, .xls or .csv file, check the columns it recognized and pick the chart. The animation runs in your own browser: the spreadsheet is not sent to any server.

01

Step by step

  1. Answer the opening questions

    Four quick questions (area, audience, decision and time) help suggest the right chart and story.

  2. Add the file

    Drag the spreadsheet (.xlsx, .xls, .csv or .tsv) or pick the file. If it has several sheets, Datavix gives each one a role and you can change it.

  3. Check how the columns were read

    Each column gets a role: date, category, number, free text, identifier or yes/no. You confirm or correct it before generating.

  4. See the chart and edit

    Datavix suggests a chart and shows the alternatives. Change chart, palette, background and fonts. Highlights come with “see calculation”.

  5. Export

    Interactive HTML, PNG, a ZIP with one PNG per step, PowerPoint (.pptx) or MP4 video.

02

Which spreadsheet works best

Any table works: Datavix decides the path from the kind of information in each column, not from the file name. The shape of your data defines which charts are available.

Data shape and available charts
If the spreadsheet has…Datavix offers…
A date and a valueLine and area
A category and a valueBars, horizontal bars and treemap
A date, 3 or more series and 4 or more periodsBar chart race
Two number columnsScatter (and bubbles, with a third)

Latitude, longitude and a valueIsoline map
A free-text column (10 rows or more)Word cloud

03

What Datavix recognizes on its own

  • Dates and numbers written in Portuguese or English.
  • Identifier, free text and yes/no columns, which do not become an axis by mistake.
  • Personal data (email, ID numbers, phone, people’s names): kept out of the charts and the presentation by default, with a warning. You can include the column.
  • Empty columns, or columns with a single value, drop out on their own.
  • Similar labels (“Purple” and “PURPLE”) are suggested for merging. You decide, and the original spreadsheet does not change.

04

Limits and care

  • Large spreadsheets (tens of MB) can take about a minute to open. If it is very large, save only the sheet and the columns that matter, or export it as .csv.
  • Above 3,000 rows, the radial tree groups records by period, entity and category.
  • The numbers in the chart come only from the spreadsheet. If the data does not support a sentence, it does not appear.
  • Datavix shows the charts your data supports. The others appear dimmed, with the reason.

05

Quick questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Datavix runs in the browser. If your browser offers it, you can install it as an app.

Does the spreadsheet go to a server?

No. It is read and processed in your browser. See the privacy policy.

Does it work with Excel and CSV?

Yes: .xlsx, .xls, .csv and .tsv.

Can I use the result offline?

Yes. The exported HTML is a single file and opens without internet.
